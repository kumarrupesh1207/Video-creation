# Long episodes (16:9) must survive the daily NIM cap: resumable panels

Problem: a ~45-panel episode needs ~45+ NIM credits but the daily cap is 25; a single-run renderer would either overspend or lose its work.
Design (`aurora/episode.py`): `find_or_plan` researches the episode once (outline + one Gemini call per act -> `Episode` schema, locked cast, analytics winners in the prompt, deterministic fallback without a key) and stores it in `outputs/episodes/<channel>-epNN-<slug>/state.json`. `generate_panels` makes the missing panels one by one with NIM only (switchboard restricted to `nvidia_nim`, ComfyUI OFF), counts EVERY call (also blocked/regenerated ones) in `outputs/costs/nvidia_nim-credits.json`, stops when `credits_today + 1 > cap`, and saves state after each panel; existing panels are never regenerated. Running the same command again continues (`find_or_plan` resumes the in-progress episode). When all panels exist: `render_episode` renders act by act (cached `work/actN.mp4`/`.json`, 1920x1080 via `comic.frame()`), concatenates with stream copy, writes SRT (from narration timings), chapters (act starts, first at 0:00), a 1280x720 thumbnail (best panel + big title away from the face) and the sidecar; `cut_shorts` makes 3-5 vertical 30 s Shorts from the same panels (hook panel first, subject-aware 9:16 crops, end card "Full story on the channel").
Resume plan for 45 panels at 25 credits/day: day 1 = 25 panels, day 2 = 20 panels + render (+ Shorts). Retries are credit-aware: one blocked-panel retry (new seed, softened prompt) and at most 4 drawn-bubble regenerations per episode.
Gotchas: the frame size is switched with `with comic.frame(1920, 1080):` (module globals in comic.py/comic_layout.py; `SAFE` is recomputed: landscape 4% sides, 10% bottom); `best_spot(safe=None)` must read `SAFE` at call time (a default argument froze it once); stream-copy concat makes the average fps 23.94, which is inside the validator's 0.1 tolerance.
