# Comic text covered the characters -> segmentation-aware placement on full-bleed art

Problem: speech bubbles and the narration box sat on faces/characters, sometimes under the Shorts/Reels UI.
What did NOT work: (1) face boxes alone (OpenCV anime/Haar cascades) miss masked, hooded, dark and side-on faces; (2) a "reserved bands" layout (top narration band, cropped art window, dialogue band) fixed the overlap but the USER does not want cropped art: they want the full-bleed 9:16 image visible. The band code still exists behind `comic.layout.bands: true` (default false).
Fix (default): `aurora/comic_layout.py` builds an occupancy grid of "where the characters are" per panel: the `rembg` `isnet-anime` foreground mask (`foreground_mask`; pip `rembg`, the 176 MB model downloads once to `~/.rembg/models/`, ~3 s per panel on CPU) plus the face boxes (nagadomi `lbpcascade_animeface`, MIT, `assets/cascades/`). `occupancy_over_motion` warps it through the Ken-Burns zoom/pan at several moments and takes the union, so the free area stays free during the move. `best_spot` scans the safe area (not the right ~12%, bottom ~18% or edge margins) for the placement with the least overlap (zero preferred; head/upper body = top ~35% of the mask counts 3x); ties go to the top, then the sides. `comic._place_text` tries font sizes 54..30 and narrower wrap widths and keeps the best score (overlap + a small penalty per shrink step), so text never gets cut off and never becomes tiny to squeeze into a gap. The bubble tail starts at the bubble edge facing the speaker's head and stops before it reaches the character.
Also kept: NIM ignores negative prompts, so the positive prompt says wordless/no lettering and `has_drawn_bubble()` regenerates a panel once (counted credit, max 2 per reel); narration capped at ~12-13 words (clean cut) so reels stay ~30 s; final encode crf 23, maxrate 6M.
How to recognise: text over a face/head in a frame; tests `tests/test_comic_layout.py` (synthetic masks: zero overlap, safe zones, shrink-to-fit) and the band tests (bands=True).
Limits: when a close-up fills the whole frame no clean spot exists; the placer then avoids the head/upper body and accepts overlap on the body. Text the image model drew into the picture itself is baked in and can only be avoided or regenerated.
