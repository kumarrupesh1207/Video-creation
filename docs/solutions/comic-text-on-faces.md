# Comic text covered the characters (face detection is not enough) -> reserved bands

Problem: speech bubbles and the narration box sat on faces. A face-aware placer (OpenCV anime/Haar cascades + edge-density fallback, mapped through the Ken-Burns move) still failed on masked, hooded, dark or side-on faces, put a box on the villain's head, cut a bottom box off, and left text under the Shorts/Reels player UI.
Lesson: never overlay text on the art. Detection can only guess where subjects are; a reserved layout cannot overlap them.
Fix (`aurora/comic.py`, `aurora/comic_layout.py::Layout`, config `comic.layout`): fixed bands in the 1080x1920 frame: top band (y 0-330) narration box | art window (y 330-1500, full width) where the image and Ken Burns live | dialogue band (y 1500-1690) speech bubble, tail pointing UP into the art toward the speaker | empty bottom safe zone (platform UI covers the bottom ~15-20%) and an empty 120 px right strip. Cover/end cards put their title in the top band and subtitle in the dialogue band. Text auto-fits (`fit_text`: wrap, balanced last line, shrink the font, ellipsis as the last resort; never cut off). Face detection (nagadomi `lbpcascade_animeface`, MIT, `assets/cascades/`) now only picks the tail's left/right side (`speaker_x`); a miss means a centred tail.
Also kept: NIM ignores negative prompts, so the positive prompt says wordless/no lettering and `has_drawn_bubble()` regenerates a panel once (counted credit, max 2 per reel); narration capped at ~12-13 words (clean cut, no dangling "a/the/in") so reels stay ~30 s; final encode crf 23, maxrate 6M.
How to recognise: text overlapping a face or the player UI area in a frame; tests `tests/test_comic_layout.py` assert text pixels stay in their bands, off the art window, the right strip and the bottom safe zone.
Note: text the image model drew into the picture itself is baked in and can only be avoided or regenerated.
