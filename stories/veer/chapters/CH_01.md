# VEER — Chapter 1: "The Face in the Stone"
Arc 1 · Awakening · 10 parts (~30 s each, 9:16) · English dialogue
Format per line: **SPEAKER** (emotion): "line". NARRATOR = calm voice, only for verses/time cards.

---

## Part 1 — "Navkashi"
1. Wide: neon towers above ancient ghats at dawn; river mist. Caption card: *Navkashi. The city built on forgotten temples.*
2. Veer on scaffolding inside a half-buried temple, brushing dust off a carving.
   **VEER** (tired, to himself): "Six more panels and I can finally pay Meera's school fees."
3. His phone buzzes — Meera's drawing of the two of them as superheroes.
   **MEERA** (voice message, cheerful): "Bhaiya! Don't fall off the wall again!"
4. Veer smiles, then freezes — a deep drumbeat only he can hear. His eyes flash gold for one frame.
   **VEER** (whisper): "…Not again."
End card: *Part 2 tomorrow*

## Part 2 — "The Drum"
1. Kabir drops down on a rope, drone hovering.
   **KABIR** (grinning): "You're talking to walls again, Veer."
2. **VEER**: "The wall's better company."
3. Veer's brush reveals ancient script; for one heartbeat it glows and becomes readable.
   **VEER** (stunned): "*'When the seventh river forgets…'* — Kabir, I can READ this."
4. **KABIR** (laughing): "Sure. And I'm the Prime Minister."
5. Close-up: the glow fades. Veer's hand is shaking.

## Part 3 — "The Face"
1. Behind the carving, a hollow — Veer breaks through into a sealed chamber.
2. Torchlight on a giant relief: a warrior with a trident of light, standing beside a flood.
3. Kabir's torch rises to the warrior's face.
   **KABIR** (very quiet): "…Veer. Bro. Look at its face."
4. Full panel: **the carving has Veer's face.** The silver streak in his hair is carved too.
   **VEER** (breathless): "That's… five thousand years old."
Cliffhanger card: *"What are we actually?"*

## Part 4 — "The Librarian"
1. Night. Veer and Kabir carry rubbings to the ruined Navkashi library.
2. An old man in a shawl, lamp in hand — **ACHARYA VYOM**.
   **VYOM** (calm, knowing): "You found the Varun panel. I wondered which life would bring you back."
3. **VEER** (defensive): "Which *life*? Sir, I'm just a restorer."
4. **VYOM** (gently): "Then restore this." He hands Veer a palm-leaf manuscript wrapped in red cloth.
5. Insert: the leaf title glows — *Smriti Sutra*.

## Part 5 — "The Verse"
1. Parchment box. **NARRATOR** (verse, slow): *"When the girl who draws the gods sleeps, she will wake as the one who sang to them."*
2. **VEER** (half-laughing): "Riddles. Great."
3. **KABIR** (reading his phone, worried): "Veer… why is Meera calling you twenty times?"
4. Phone screen: 20 missed calls — "Meera ❤".
5. Veer running through neon rain.

## Part 6 — "Meera"
1. Their small flat. Meera sits up in bed, eyes glowing silver, drawing ancient symbols on the wall with her finger.
2. **VEER** (scared, soft): "Meera? It's me. Bhaiya."
3. **MEERA** (in an older, echoing voice): "I do not know you, warrior. Where is the river temple? Where are my people?"
4. Veer's heart breaks — close-up of his eyes.
   **VEER**: "You're… you're twelve. You hate mornings. You draw dragons with moustaches."
5. Meera blinks, confused — for a second her own eyes return, then vanish.

## Part 7 — "Smriti-flood"
1. Outside, sirens. Across the street, a man screams in a language nobody speaks anymore.
2. TV news: **ANCHOR**: "Hundreds in Navkashi report 'memories that aren't theirs.' Doctors call it mass hysteria."
3. Tara Iyer bursts in with Vyom, holding a tablet of glowing translations.
   **TARA** (sharp): "It's not hysteria. It's a script older than Sanskrit — and it's waking up in people's heads."
4. **VEER**: "Who are you?"
   **TARA** (not looking at him): "Someone who's been waiting for you to remember. Long story."

## Part 8 — "The Herald"
1. The river rises unnaturally in the night; the water forms a tall figure with a face of currents — **JALAGHNA**.
   **JALAGHNA** (deep, echoing): "The Veil is thinning, Varun. My lord sends his regards."
2. **VEER**: "I'm not Varun!"
3. **JALAGHNA** (amused): "No. You are the boy who borrowed his face."
4. A wave of black water rushes toward the ghats full of people.

## Part 9 — "Jagrit"
1. Veer jumps in front of the crowd; the war-drum pounds; his eyes blaze gold.
2. **VYOM** (shouting): "Don't fight it, Veer! Remember!"
3. Flash panel: a memory — a First-Age battlefield, a sky-vessel, his own hand on a trident of light.
4. Veer's palm erupts with light; the wave splits in two around the crowd.
   **VEER** (roaring): "NOT — MY — CITY!"
5. Cost panel: a memory of Meera's 8th birthday cracks like glass and disappears from his mind.

## Part 10 — "The Price"
1. Dawn. The water is calm. Jalaghna is gone; the crowd stares at Veer.
2. **KABIR** (awed, shaky): "Bro… you split a river."
3. Veer, kneeling, confused: **VEER**: "Kabir… when is Meera's birthday?"
   **KABIR**: "…June 12th. Why?"
   **VEER** (horrified, whisper): "I don't remember it."
4. **VYOM** (grave): "Every memory you wake costs one you have. That is the Vinimay, child. Power is paid in yourself."
5. Final panel: far away, on a mountain of stone ruins, **SMARAN** opens burning eyes.
   **SMARAN**: "Brother. You've finally woken."
End card: *Chapter 1 — End. Chapter 2: "The Girl Who Sang to Gods."*
